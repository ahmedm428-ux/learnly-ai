Cellular Respiration & ATP Production

Generated just now

Key Concept: Cellular respiration is the set of metabolic reactions that take place in organisms' cells to convert chemical energy from nutrients into ATP (adenosine triphosphate).

The Three Main Stages:

  • Glycolysis: Occurs in the cytoplasm; breaks down glucose into 2 pyruvate molecules.
  • Krebs Cycle (Citric Acid Cycle): Occurs in the mitochondrial matrix; produces electron carriers (NADH, FADH2).
  • Electron Transport Chain (ETC): Produces the majority of ATP via oxidative phosphorylation.
